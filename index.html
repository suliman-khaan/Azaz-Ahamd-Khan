01 About the Firm

Integrated Legal, Taxation & Corporate Counsel Under One Roof

Established in 2019, Azaz & Associates LLP is a Limited Liability Partnership providing comprehensive Legal, Tax & Corporate Consultancy Services. We are among the first professional firms in the region to bring litigation, taxation, and corporate regulatory advisory together under one roof—making dependable counsel accessible and streamlined for individuals, businesses, companies, and organizations.

Our practice spans Civil, Criminal, Family, and Service Matters, including specialized representation in FIA and NCCIA related proceedings. Alongside litigation, we maintain a dedicated regulatory and corporate desk covering Income Tax, Sales Tax, FBR matters, SECP corporate services, Trademark Registration, IPO Pakistan matters, Import & Export Licensing, and ongoing Corporate Compliance.

Our multidisciplinary team of Advocates, Tax Consultants, Corporate Consultants, Legal Consultants, and Associates serves clients throughout Pakistan and advises on international tax and corporate consultancy matters.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, reliable, and professional legal, tax, and corporate services under one roof, protect our clients’ interests, ensure regulatory compliance, and minimize legal and financial risks through expert guidance.

Our Vision

To become a leading and internationally recognized Legal, Tax & Corporate Consultancy Firm, known for professional excellence, integrity, client satisfaction, and reliable solutions.
